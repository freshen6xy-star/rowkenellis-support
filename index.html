Frequently asked questions

How do I send a support request?＋

Use the form in the Contact Support section. Describe the issue clearly and include only information needed to respond.

Can support ask me for my password or OTP?＋

No. Never share passwords, one-time passcodes, recovery codes, or payment details with anyone claiming to provide support.

No. Rowkenellis is an independent website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
